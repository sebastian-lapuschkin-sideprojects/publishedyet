Has it been published yet?

  • Keep your BibTeX files up to date with minimal effort.
  • Benefit from a shared database of cited papers, and add to it.
  • Cite the correct, peer-reviewed works.

Paste arXiv, bioRxiv or OpenReview ids, or a whole BibTeX file. Preprints get replaced by their published versions; your keys stay.

Everything runs in your browser; your file is not uploaded anywhere. Only preprint ids and the titles of those preprints leave your computer: to this site's database and to DBLP, OpenAlex, Crossref, bioRxiv and PMLR. How it works
